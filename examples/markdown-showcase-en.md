# Before Dawn, We Drove to the Sea

At 10:47 Friday night, Aya texted, "Sunrise tomorrow?" I was fully committed to ~~sleeping in~~. Three minutes later: **I'm in.** We dropped a pin on [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/), set a `4:20 a.m.` alarm, and started looking forward to _a morning that did not exist yet_.

> "Dawn reaches the water before it reaches us."

## Before We Left

The streets belonged to delivery vans. At the only shop still glowing, we bought coffee and mints.

| Time      | Stop        | What we found                    |
| :-------- | :---------- | :------------------------------- |
| 4:34 a.m. | Corner shop | Coffee, mints, and the night air |
| 5:16 a.m. | East pier   | Waves louder than our footsteps  |

### What We Packed

1. A jacket that could handle the wind.
2. A blank notebook with no obligation to be filled.

- We hoped to bring home the sea air and the slow brightening of the sky.
- We were happy to leave behind the habit of rushing and whatever had gone wrong yesterday.

> [!TIP] A Small Sunrise Trick
> Do not stare at the horizon the whole time. Turn around now and then; the clouds behind you are changing color too.
